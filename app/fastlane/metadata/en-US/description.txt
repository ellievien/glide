Glide sends files directly between your devices over the local network — no cloud, no account, no size limits.

Open Glide on two devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network and they find each other automatically. Pick a photo, video, document, or any other file, choose the nearby device, and it transfers straight to it — similar to how AirDrop works between Apple devices, except Glide also works with Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux, so you can send between any mix of platforms.

How it works:
• Automatic discovery of nearby devices on the same network
• Direct device-to-device transfer — your files never pass through a server
• Send photos and videos, files and folders, or plain text and links
• Transfer history so you can find what you recently sent or received
• Works across iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux

Privacy by design:
Glide doesn't have user accounts, doesn't run analytics or ad tracking, and doesn't send your files or metadata anywhere outside your local network. What you share stays between your devices.

Glide is free to use, with no in-app purchases and no ads.
