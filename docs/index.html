Glide

Send files directly between your devices over your own Wi-Fi — no cloud, no account, no size limits.

Open Glide on two devices connected to the same network and they find each other automatically. Pick a photo, video, document, or any other file, choose the nearby device, and it transfers straight to it. Works across iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux.

  • Direct device-to-device transfer — your files never pass through a server
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ad tracking
  • Free, with no in-app purchases
