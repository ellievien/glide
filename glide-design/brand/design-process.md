# Glide design process

Built-in image generation was used for concept exploration. The selected paired-wing direction was rebuilt as clean editable SVG geometry. The final icon backgrounds, wordmark outlines, and exports were constructed from those vector masters. No fallback image-generation CLI was used.

## Concept exploration prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a refined black-and-white logo concept sheet for an app named Glide, a cross-platform alternative to AirDrop for nearby file sharing. This is a design exploration sheet with three genuinely different candidate marks, each next to the exact word Glide in carefully spaced modern sans-serif lettering. Explore 1) a flowing uppercase G constructed from a broad smooth ribbon with an opening suggesting forward motion, 2) two offset soft geometric wings or file shapes passing each other, 3) a compact continuous path suggesting a connection between two devices. Prefer abstract, memorable, clean silhouettes and a small number of editable-vector-friendly shapes. Flat black artwork on pure white, generous whitespace, calm precise professional tech brand. No effects, gradients, mockups, 3D, shadows, decorative lines, arrows inside circles, AirDrop concentric circles, Apple marks, stock paper planes, or slogans. Each candidate should be plausible at app-icon size. Three tidy rows, no text except Glide repeated once in each row. Landscape design presentation.

## Refined icon concept prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final app icon concept for Glide, a cross-platform nearby file transfer app. Create one impeccable minimalist app icon filling a square canvas edge to edge with fully opaque rich cobalt blue, approximately #355CF5. Center a solid white geometric symbol formed from exactly two broad parallel aerodynamic rounded file-like wings, offset diagonally, separated by a generous clean diagonal channel. Each shape is an elongated softened parallelogram with a subtly curved leading edge, suggesting two files gliding past each other. Upper shape sits slightly to the left, lower shape slightly to the right. The combined compact silhouette should feel balanced and instantly recognizable at 24px. White mark occupies about 58 percent of canvas width. Design as crisp flat vector-style geometry with solid fills. No G letter, no text, no gradients, no border, no shadows, no transparency anywhere, no mockup, no extra shapes, no paper airplane, no enclosing white circle. This is the full square source app icon; do not round the corners of the outer blue canvas.
