Why Glide

A cross-platform alternative to AirDrop, built for every device you actually own.

Cross-platform

Send between any mix of iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux — one app, every device.

Local network, no cloud

Files travel straight to the other device over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing passes through a server.

Fast

No upload, no queue, no server-imposed size limit — transfer speed is just your Wi-Fi.

Private

No accounts, no analytics, no ad tracking. What you share stays between your devices.
